Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this app for?
This app is designed for authorised Te Toka Tumai Auckland staff who need quick access to emergency procedures.
Does the app collect any personal information?
No. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. More details are available in the Privacy Policy.
How often is the content updated?
Updates are made whenever new information is provided by hospital emergency management teams.
I found incorrect or outdated information — what should I do?
Please email us and include the section or page where the issue appears so we can correct it as soon as possible.
