Privacy Policy – Emergency Procedures

Last Updated: 2025-12-05

Emergency Procedures is a reference app designed for staff at Te Toka Tumai Auckland to quickly access emergency response documents. The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information or patient data.

Data Collection

The app does not collect or transmit any personal data. This includes:

  • No user profiles or login accounts
  • No tracking or analytics
  • No communication with external servers

Device Permissions

The app may access local storage in order to display bundled reference documents (PDFs). It does not access:

  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Location
  • Microphone
  • Health or patient data

Document Content

Emergency procedures are stored directly within the app bundle. These documents do not include any personal information.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party SDKs or APIs that collect data.

Use of the App

This app is intended only for trained hospital staff. It supports but does not replace clinical judgement, and users must follow all local hospital procedures.
